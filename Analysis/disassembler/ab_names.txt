179A title_twinkle ; (x, y, d): a sparkle: 11 rounds of 4 more pixels around (x, y) (offsets DS:0936) turn white (colour 0Fh), each round a busy loop of DS:0005 x 1500; 2 ticks; the same rounds put back; d ticks; returns get_key (the title's sparkles; a key skips the credits)
18C9 fire_flicker ; (frames): the campfire in Sherwood: sprite slot 0 at (144, 121) cycles off / flame 1 / flame 2 (the sprites at CS:014C, CS:0154) each frame, showing the bottom half of the screen (present_bottom)
2529 lady_at ; (territory): the lady (0-3) whose territory it is, or -1 (never called)
3F4F draw_castles ; on the back page: a castle sprite (picture item by castle type) for every territory with a castle, at its map position (DS:0C14), and the player's campaign army at its territory (DS:0C3A); through sprite slot 0
40C6 flash_territory ; (territory, n): XORs the territory's mask on the screen n times, once a timer tick (makes it blink)
479B lady_animate ; (lady): puts her frames, the BE sprites (2000:0) item DS:0ABE[lady] + 2 up to + 5 (ladies' bases 2, 8, 14, 20), one a tick, at x DS:0AA0 / y DS:0A9E (4 bytes a lady; Anne's x + 1) (the romance scene)

4CAC sword_pose_b ; (10): the two background fighters of the Attack (record DS:0B1C) into sprite slots 4-7
